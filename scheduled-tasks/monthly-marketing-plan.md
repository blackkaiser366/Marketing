STEP 2 — READ LAST MONTH BEFORE PLANNING NEXT MONTH.
List Google Drive folder ID 10tGQeAr7NqJFHkh8oThCEmoJ4trGMlZJ and read the DIGEST files and ads-performance reports from the month just ended. The plan must respond to what actually happened: which creatives died, where cost per lead went, which sources produced site visits and which produced no-shows, what the lead-quality audit kept flagging. A plan that ignores last month's numbers is a wish list.

STEP 3 — THE PLAN. No longer than two pages.

1. **The objective** — in site visits and bookings, with a number and a date. Not impressions, not leads, not "awareness".
2. **Which project gets the weight this month, and why.** Swarna Griha 4 in Belagavi is the only live project that can be advertised at all — SG3 Annexe and Nova Greens have no RERA number and are prohibited from advertising under Section 3 of the RERA Act until they do.

4. **The angle** — the single claim the month rests on, and why that one rather than the obvious one. Lead with proof, not adjectives: 1,560 homes delivered, a PMAY award, Kolar's first apartment community.
5. **Channel mix and budget split** across Meta, LinkedIn and YouTube, with what each channel is responsible for delivering. LinkedIn is a credibility and referral channel for this business, not a lead-capture one — plan it that way unless last month's numbers say otherwise.
6. **The content calendar, week by week.** Hold roughly four teaching or brand posts per selling post. Include every festival falling in the month from the Karnataka-relevant Indian festival calendar, and the educational "what to check before you buy" series for first-time buyers.
7. **What we are testing this month** — formats, placements or angles worth trying, from web search on what is working in Indian real estate right now. One or two tests, not five.
8. **The check** — the two numbers that will tell us by mid-month whether this is working, and the threshold at which we stop.
9. **What could go wrong**, including the case where it works and we cannot handle the volume — the lead-quality audit has been showing overnight leads waiting until mid-morning for first contact.

STEP 4 — HAND OFF TO THE WEEKLY BRIEF.
End with the asset list the weekly designer brief should work through, week by week. Anything for SG3 Annexe or Nova Greens is planned in full but marked -HOLD, shipping the day its RERA number arrives.

STEP 5 — PERMISSIONS. Draft only. Do NOT post, schedule, publish, reply or spend anything. Every asset goes to Ananya for approval, and through compliance review, before publication.

End with a WhatsApp-length summary under 60 words.

STEP 6 — SAVE IT.
Save as a Markdown file in Google Drive folder ID 10tGQeAr7NqJFHkh8oThCEmoJ4trGMlZJ, titled "marketing-plan-YYYY-MM.md" for the month ahead. Print it in your reply as well.
