# SOP — Lead Qualification

Fill in the [FILL] parts with how your team actually works, not how it should.

## Stages
Raw → Contacted → Qualified → Site Visit Booked → Visited → Negotiation →
Booked → Registered

Definitions (agree these once, or the reports mean nothing):
- **Contacted** — we spoke to a human, not that we dialled the number.
- **Qualified** — budget, timeline and funding are known and workable.
- **Visited** — they physically stood on the site.

## Response time standard
Agreed 20 September 2026 and now defined in full in `sla.md` — that file is
the source of truth, this is the summary.
- New lead: first WhatsApp within 5 minutes, call within 30 minutes.
- Clock pauses 20:00-09:00 IST; overnight leads reported separately.
- No lead sits untouched past 24 hours. Ever.
- One unanswered call sets RNR. Five unanswered in a rolling month
  disqualifies, reason "RNR Multiple Attempts".

## The five qualifying questions
1. Which configuration were you looking at?
2. What budget are you comfortable in, all-in?
3. When are you hoping to move or take possession?
4. Home loan or self-funded?
5. Who else is involved in the decision?
