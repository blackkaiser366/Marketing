# SOP — The Weekly Rhythm

The system is only worth what you run on it. This is the minimum cadence.

**Monday — look back, then plan**
- `run the performance report for last week`
- Read the three recommended actions. Do at least one.

**Tuesday — content**
- `make an Instagram carousel about [topic]` (four teaching posts per selling post)
- `write WhatsApp messages for [this week's announcement]`

**Wednesday — market**
- `run a competitor sweep for [micro-market]` (fortnightly is enough)

**Thursday — sales support**
- Whatever the sales team is losing deals on this week:
  `handle the objection "[exact words the buyer used]" for [project]`

**Friday — housekeeping**
- Update `system/context/projects.md` with any price or inventory change.
  Nothing downstream is right if this file is stale.
- Refresh any portal listing older than 30 days.

**Whenever a long session produces something good**
- `turn everything we just did into a skill`

That last habit is the whole system. Three skills becomes fifteen by doing it.
