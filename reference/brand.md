"Luxury Living, Now Within Reach." Thoughtfully designed homes in high-growth
locations, built for modern families and smart investments.

we build homes that let families stop renting and start owning.

Four pillars used across the site: Accessible Luxury, Trusted Quality,
Smart Locations, Community Living.

What every project includes: gated security with controlled access, lifts and
planned parking, kids' play areas, shared community spaces, power backup and
water systems, home automation provision, guest parking, maintenance support.

## Voice

The website's voice is plain, warm and proof-led, and it works. Keep it.
Short declarative sentences. Specific numbers. Dignity rather than aspiration —
these are people buying their first home and the tone should respect that.

**This works** (site's own lines, as a reference for tone)
- Kolar had no apartment community before ours. We built first, and the town
  grew around us.
- Every unit is taken. The story is still worth knowing.
- For most families this is their first home, and that deserves to feel like
  something.

**Avoid**
- "Nestled", "oasis", "redefined", exclamation marks
- Anything implying a financial return

## Rules that never bend

- RERA number on every asset that advertises a project.
- Renders labelled "artist's impression" — the website already carries this
  disclaimer; every asset we make must carry it too.
- No AI-generated property imagery. See the note below.
- No return, rental-yield or appreciation claims.
- Sold-out projects are proof, never inventory.

## ⚠️ Note on the current website imagery

A number of images and background videos on the live site appear to be
AI-generated or AI-upscaled stock (filenames beginning "ChatGPT Image" and
"freepik_"), including some used in project contexts. Whatever is decided
about the website, **assets produced in this workspace do not use AI-generated
property imagery.** Elevations, interiors, views, amenities and locations come
from real photographs or the architect's approved renders in
`system/brand/<project>/`. If the shot doesn't exist, we say which photo needs
taking. Raise this with the owner once — it is a RERA and consumer-protection
exposure, not a design preference.
