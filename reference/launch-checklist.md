# SOP — New Project Launch

Work down this list. Each line is a command you can paste.

**Two weeks before**
1. Add the project to `system/context/projects.md` — every field, RERA included.
2. Put photos, approved renders, floor plans and the price sheet in
   `system/brand/<project>/`.
3. `plan the launch campaign for [project]`  → campaign-strategist
4. `run a competitor sweep for [micro-market]`

**One week before**
5. `make a brochure for [project]`
6. `build a landing page for [project]`
7. `write Meta ads for [project]`
8. `review all of these for compliance`  → compliance-reviewer

**Launch week**
9. `write the WhatsApp first reply and broadcast for [project]`
10. `build the site visit follow-up sequence for [project]`
11. `write the 99acres and Housing listings for [project]`
12. Brief the sales team on the follow-up sequence before the ads go live.
    Leads arriving into an unprepared team is the most expensive mistake here.

**72 hours after launch**
13. `run the performance report for the last 3 days`
14. Kill what isn't producing site visits. Not leads — visits.
